title=Tien's Last Seen Where
description=Forgot where you saw that hammer? Type an item's name and see every container and floor where your character saw it, then follow an arrow on the floor back to it.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Only what you have seen:[/b] a container counts once you have looked inside it in the loot window. Containers you never opened are never searched, and a padlocked crate counts only for someone who can open it (key in hand), as in the loot window. Items on the floor count once they are in sight (small ones only up close, and in the dark only within reach), or when search mode spots them.
description=[*][b]Search as you type:[/b] results are grouped by item, then by place, in columns you can resize and sort by: a small arrow pointing the way, the place and room, how far and which floor, how long ago it was seen, who found it (you, or the other player by name, as username or character name: switch at the bottom of the window) and how many. The window remembers its size, position and columns.
description=[*][b]Where to look:[/b] everywhere, this building, nearby, or on you.
description=[*][b]Show[/b] puts an arrow on the floor at your feet, aimed at the place, and highlights the container (or the items on the floor) in the arrow colour. On another floor it points to the stairs first. [b]Go there[/b] walks you to it and opens the loot window on it. [b]Take[/b] picks the item up once it is within reach.
description=[*][b]Memory follows the character:[/b] kept on the server and forgotten when the character dies.
description=[*][b]Who shares with whom:[/b] finding works like word of mouth, you only learn from players who would tell you. With factions on, only faction members share, and only with their own faction (a player in no faction shares with nobody). With PVP and factions off, everyone shares with everyone. With PVP on and factions off, nobody shares. Safehouses never count, and whoever told you is always named.
description=[*][b]Privacy:[/b] right-click a container (in the world or its loot window button) or an item and pick Privacy, or use the window's Privacy tab, to mark it private to you; a container can be marked even while it is empty. What you saw of it is left out of other players' searches; anyone else who sees it remembers and shares it as usual. A marked bag hides everything in it, and your own private things show as "You (private)" in your results. If someone from your faction finds it, you hear who and when; other factions never tell you. Forgotten when you die. Admins can tick a box to see private things.
description=[*][b]Manage (admins):[/b] a Manage tab lists every player who remembers something. Pick one to see what they remember, then make them forget an item everywhere, at single places, or everything. Only their own memory by default; tick "Also from shared knowledge" to also remove what they saw last from what Opened by anyone finds. Their privacy marks are kept, and seeing something again brings it back.
description=[*][b]Gone is gone:[/b] walk past a container, corpse, bag or floor spot you remember that is no longer there (smashed, burnt, moved, picked up) and it is forgotten.
description=[/list]
