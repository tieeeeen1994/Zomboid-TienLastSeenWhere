title=Tien's Last Seen Where
description=Forgot where you saw that hammer? Type an item's name and see every container and floor where your character saw it, then follow an arrow on the floor back to it.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Only what you have seen:[/b] a container counts once you have looked inside it in the loot window. Containers you never opened are never searched. Items on the floor count once they are in sight (small ones only up close, and in the dark only within reach), or when search mode spots them.
description=[*][b]Search as you type:[/b] results are grouped by item, then by place, in columns you can resize and sort by: a small arrow pointing the way, the place and room, how far and which floor, how long ago it was seen, who found it (you, your faction or safehouse by name, or someone) and how many. The window remembers its size, position and columns.
description=[*][b]Where to look:[/b] everywhere, this building, nearby, or on you.
description=[*][b]Show[/b] puts an arrow on the floor at your feet, aimed at the place, and highlights the container (or the items on the floor) in the arrow colour. On another floor it points to the stairs first. [b]Go there[/b] walks you to it and opens the loot window on it. [b]Take[/b] picks the item up once it is within reach.
description=[*][b]Memory follows the character:[/b] kept on the server and forgotten when the character dies.
description=[*][b]Privacy:[/b] right-click a container (in the world or its loot window button) or an item and pick Privacy, or use the window's Privacy tab, to mark it private to you or to your faction and safehouse; a container can be marked even while it is empty. What you saw of it is left out of other players' searches; anyone else who sees it remembers and shares it as usual. A marked bag hides everything in it, and your own private things show as "You (private)" in your results. If someone from your faction or safehouse finds it, you hear who and when; other factions never tell you. Forgotten when you die. Admins can tick a box to see private things.
description=[*][b]Gone is gone:[/b] walk past a container, corpse, bag or floor spot you remember that is no longer there (smashed, burnt, moved, picked up) and it is forgotten.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=Open the search with the magnifier button on the left sidebar, under Inventory, or bind a key to [i]Find an item you have seen[/i] in Key Bindings (not bound by default).
description=Per player, in Settings > Mods: what to search (what I have seen, shared with my faction and safehouse, or opened by anyone; also in the search window), the arrow colour and the arrow size.
description=Sandbox: one search rule for everyone (default: each player's choice), items on the floor (in sight, within reach, off), small item distance (default 4 tiles), forget after N days (default never), keep memories fresh when opened or also nearby (default when opened), privacy (default on), found by shows usernames or character names (default usernames).
