description=Forgot where you saw that saw? Type an item's name and see every container and floor where your character saw it, then follow an arrow on the floor back to it.

description=[list]
description=[*][b]Only what you have seen:[/b] a container counts once you have looked inside it in the loot window. Containers you never opened are never searched. Items on the floor count once they are in sight (small ones only up close, and in the dark only within reach), or when search mode spots them.
description=[*][b]Search as you type:[/b] results are grouped by item, then by place, with a small arrow pointing the way, the place and room, how far, which floor and how long ago you saw it.
description=[*][b]Where to look:[/b] everywhere, this building, nearby, or on you.
description=[*][b]Show[/b] puts an arrow on the floor at your feet, aimed at the place, and marks the square. On another floor it points to the stairs first. [b]Go there[/b] walks you to it and opens the loot window on it. [b]Take[/b] picks the item up once it is within reach.
description=[*][b]Memory follows the character:[/b] kept on the server and forgotten when the character dies.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=Key: [i]Find an item you have seen[/i] in Key Bindings, Ctrl + F by default.
description=Per player, in Settings > Mods: what to search (what I have seen, shared with my faction and safehouse, opened by anyone, or everything) and the arrow colour.
description=Sandbox: one search rule for everyone (default: each player's choice), items on the floor (in sight, within reach, off), small item distance (default 4 tiles), forget after N days (default never).
